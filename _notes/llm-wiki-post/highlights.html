Four Methodological Gaps in Field Potential Analysis of hiPSC-Cardiomyocytes
A Narrative Review of Multielectrode Array Studies Since 2022
  • MEA tools define FPD differently; their FPD values are not directly comparable
  • FPD drifts over days in culture even under pacing
  • No MEA-derived reference values for hiPSC-CM maturation were found
  • Machine learning on MEA parameters may learn analysis, not cell, differences
  • Raw MEA signals mix cell, tissue and device; the separated cellular signal could be a useful input in its own right
FPD, field potential duration; hiPSC-CM, human induced pluripotent stem cell–derived cardiomyocyte; MEA, multielectrode array.
